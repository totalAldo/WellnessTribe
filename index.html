Make room for your wellbeing.

Choose meaningful practices, notice your progress, and reflect at your own pace. Wellness Tribe brings four light-fantasy elements to a private, personal wellness practice.

  • Sage

    Reflection, meaning, and emotional care.

  • Scholar

    Learning, meaningful work, and growth.

  • Warrior

    Accessible movement and nutrition.

  • Alchemist

    Creativity, leisure, and connection.

Personalize a practice, record it on Today or catch up later, and plan a reward for balanced progress. Your personal records are stored locally. A guided tour and an isolated demo help you explore.

This faculty-review beta uses draft activities and points inspired by the Adlerian Indivisible Self model of wellness. All group interactions are simulated. The app does not provide clinical assessments or enroll participants in research.
