How can we help?

For app help, beta feedback, or privacy questions, contact Aldo Gonzalez at wellnesstribe@totalaldo.com.

Get started

Choose Explore Demo for temporary examples, or Start Fresh for a personal practice saved on your device. A returning player sees Continue your practice. Settings lets you return to Welcome or reset the demo. Resetting a demo leaves personal records intact.

Find the guide

Open the question-mark button on a main screen, or Settings → Guide & help. The replayable tour uses separate examples and never changes your real practice history.

Record or correct a practice

Complete a selected practice on Today, or use Catch up for today and the preceding six calendar days. Each practice earns credit once per local day. Use Undo to correct a completion. Editing a practice affects future awards; earlier records keep their original points and element.

Understand levels and rewards

A level unlocks after another 100 points in every element. Excess points carry forward and missed days never subtract points. Rewards are your personal plans; there are no purchases or redemption services.

Keep reflections private

Reflections in Journey are private local records. Sharing previews show only content you explicitly choose. All tribe members and group interactions are simulated; no content is sent to another person.

Change appearance

In Settings → Display → Appearance, choose System, Light, or Dark. The appearance menu on Welcome offers the same choices.

Report an issue

Use TestFlight feedback or email support. Include your device, iOS or iPadOS version, the app version/build shown in Settings, and the steps that led to the issue. Choose what personal information to include in your feedback.

Manage local records

You can delete individual reflections in Journey. Deleting the app removes its local records and preferences; offloading retains them. iOS or iPadOS may include app data in device backups, which you manage separately in device settings.

Read the privacy policy for details about local data, optional support email, and Apple’s TestFlight information.
