Privacy policy

Updated October 15, 2026 · Developer: Aldo Gonzalez

Your local practice

Wellness Tribe stores your optional display name, practices, completion history, reflections, reward plans, meeting settings, and faculty edits locally. Appearance, haptics, and guide preferences are saved on your device. The app has no account, advertising, analytics SDK, or developer-operated server, and does not transmit these records to the developer.

Demo and sharing previews

Explore Demo uses temporary, isolated example data that resets when you leave or reset the demo. All tribe members and group interactions are simulated. Selecting content for a sharing preview displays it locally; it does not send it to another person.

Backups and removing data

iOS and iPadOS may include local app data in device backups according to your backup settings. The app does not operate its own cloud synchronization service. You can delete individual reflections in Journey. To remove the app’s local records and preferences, delete Wellness Tribe from your device; offloading the app retains its data. Manage any device backups separately in your device settings.

Optional links and support

Research and licensing links open external websites under those sites’ privacy policies. Emailing support is optional and opens your email app. If you send an email, the developer receives your email address and the content you choose to send, and uses them to respond and resolve your request. Support correspondence is retained only as needed to handle your request and meet applicable recordkeeping obligations. You can request deletion by emailing support. Your email provider also handles the message under its own policies.

TestFlight

When you install this beta through TestFlight, Apple collects and shares testing information with the developer, including usage and crash information and feedback you choose to submit, under Apple’s TestFlight terms and privacy information. This is separate from your private wellness records; the app does not attach those records to feedback.

Wellness and research

This beta supports personal wellness reflection and faculty review. It does not enroll you in a study, collect research data, provide clinical assessments, or offer medical diagnosis or treatment. Standardized questionnaires are not included.

Changes and contact

This policy describes this offline beta. Changes to data handling will be reflected in an updated policy. Contact Aldo Gonzalez at wellnesstribe@totalaldo.com with privacy or support questions.
